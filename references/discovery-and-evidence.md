# Discovery and evidence discipline

Primary sources read:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805?hl=en — Search matches title, description and video content to queries plus engagement for the search. Home/Suggested are personalized. Tags mainly spelling variants. Title changes influence viewer response, not a guaranteed ranking reset.
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?hl=en — no favored video format; selection, average view duration, average percentage viewed and satisfaction signals matter. Topic interest, competition and seasonality affect impressions.
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en — distinguish searchable and intriguing titles, be succinct/accurate, important words early, inspect audience/traffic-specific performance. Platform advice is not a causal guarantee.

## Before drafting
Declare a primary discovery hypothesis (search-led, recommendation-led, or deliberately hybrid), one viewer task, and concrete payoff. This is editorial intent, not a forecast of actual traffic. Do not force every video into the same hook: search viewers may need a direct task/result; recommendation viewers may need context showing why the problem matters.

Use Pizza Method to generate candidates, not evidence. Mark Expertise only with supporting owner material; Inquiry with actual question receipts; High Demand with research. CJ's requested topic is owner direction, not proof audience demand exists.

## Avoid survivorship bias
A viral example is a lead to investigate, not a template to canonize. Inspect multiple comparable uploads, including ordinary/weak ones. Record URL, observation date, age, length, format, topic, public metrics and opening transcript. Do not infer private CTR/retention from views, subscribers or comments. Within-channel comparisons still have topic/timing/audience confounders. A prominent creator's advice remains attributed practitioner judgment unless independently supported.

## Diagnose before prescribing
Low impressions alone cannot prove weak SEO or a bad hook. CTR needs surface/audience context. High CTR with early drop suggests promise mismatch or opening friction to inspect, not an automatic diagnosis. Spikes may mean confusion. Tutorial exits can mean task completion; assess payoff timing and feedback. Do not pad videos to force retention or use universal target percentages without a defensible comparison group.

## Learning record
Keep: hypothesis -> precise creative change -> observed outcome -> alternative explanations -> keep/test/reject decision. Separate CJ preference, reviewer judgment, footage observations and audience results. Do not claim to have cracked YouTube or imply background learning continues without actual execution.

## Production rule
A numbered title is an editorial container, not an algorithm advantage. Keep CJ's concrete numbered series preference while verifying distinct useful items. Title + thumbnail earn accurate interest; opening confirms it; demonstration delivers; close offers a relevant next action. Preserve the frozen recorded draft.
