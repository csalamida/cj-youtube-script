# Creator-script case study: structure, not virality proof

## Source boundary
CJ supplied Facebook post text attributing packaging and scripting work to Saira Chaudhry, Sam Charles, and One Billion Media. Four matched public video transcripts were inspected: VYVE Wellness [masterclass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lI_lCngfvcI), [every type](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pixiXJaOi2A), [GHK-Cu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SHTdNGUYlps), and Dr David Jockers' [7-day cortisol-belly video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I115pr36Ywo). The post's role and performance claims are attributed, not independently established. Transcripts are auto-generated; medical statements are not fact-checked and must not become HighLevel claims. Titles and thumbnails were matched to the shared images; full video editing and private analytics were not audited.

## Transferable editorial moves
- Use two valid ideation entries: a viewer task can lead to a truthful thumbnail, or a striking *real, filmable* thumbnail concept can suggest a viewer task. Neither replaces demand, evidence, title-promise, or demo checks. A borrowed pose is inspiration, not proof. Never fabricate a HighLevel UI or result.
- Choose a structure that matches the lesson, rather than a fixed retention template: a **decision framework** (masterclass, three floors, ~02:23 and applied ~11:00), **one meaningful contrast** (GHK-Cu, topical vs other routes, ~04:37), **distinct alternatives** (a 'ways' tutorial), or **cumulative steps** (7-day video, first action ~00:43, day-seven integration ~19:20). Alternatives are not cumulative steps; don't pretend one method must precede another.
- Transitions answer the next viewer question or expose a limitation of the previous step. Address specific objections using verified CJ experience or clearly labeled hypothetical UI situations. Give the first useful action/result early; a diagnostic explainer may spend longer on context than a search-led screen tutorial.
- Let the CTA follow a real lesson boundary or next task, not a generic pitch. Do not manufacture client/patient stories or first-person outcomes for CJ.
- Run the **promise-honesty check** across thumbnail, title, opening, first real visual, demonstrated payoff, caveat, and ending. The seven-day video's title/scan-like thumbnail suggests rapid fat loss, while its ~22:05 caveat says visceral fat takes 8–12 weeks. High reach does not excuse a delayed limitation or a result the video cannot prove.

## Evidence and learning limit
Outlier posts and visible view totals do not isolate scripting, positioning, thumbnail, topic, distribution, existing audience, or timing effects. Sample ordinary/weak comparable uploads, not only selected winners. Since August 24, 2026, [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) says views count at playback start across formats; engaged views are viewers who stay beyond the initial seconds (excluding loops). Do not infer engaged views or qualified leads from public views or watch hours. After CJ publishes, use authorized intro/retention, traffic source, average view duration, and downstream feedback; until then label edits editorial hypotheses, not proven performance improvements.
