## Generate ideas before scripting
Use territory -> source -> angle -> concrete viewer task -> numbered packaging. CJ favors specific '5 ways' style working titles, not generic 'Ways to...' menus. Propose a number at ideation time; mark it provisional internally until distinct points are verified. Never pad or fabricate methods to keep the number. Numbered formats can include ways, mistakes, checks, questions, or examples; not all must compare UI entry points.
Separate demand evidence from editorial hypotheses. Likes/views are useful leads but not isolated proof of topic demand; inspect channel context, age and several comparable examples. Stories require CJ's real material.

## Mandatory opening package
Draft three genuinely different approaches (result-first, recognizable problem, meaningful contrast). Each includes:
- Specific working title and truthful thumbnail concept.
- Spoken opening: short, speakable, concrete. Identify task and reason to stay, not just announce the video agenda.
- On-screen text hook: a short complementary idea, not a paragraph of subtitles.
- Visual/edit hook: exact visible starting image, change/action and transition into the first useful demonstration. Real UI or clearly labeled conceptual graphics; no fabricated results.
- Payoff contract and evidence/asset requirements.
Choose by clarity, audience fit, curiosity with payoff, filmability and naturalness. These are editorial judgments, not predicted retention scores.

Avoid 'nobody tells you', 'you're doing it wrong', invented speed claims, 'number five will shock you', and withholding the actual answer solely to prolong watch time. A numbered title is packaging; it is not the entire opening. Let the viewer see something useful early. Text, voice and edit should express the same promise without all repeating identical wording.

## Learning loop
Keep winning/losing draft comparisons and CJ's reason for preference. After footage exists, assess delivery separately. After publication and authorized analytics access, inspect intro retention, dips, title/thumbnail match and audience context; do not attribute outcomes to one hook variable without evidence. Preserve finished recording drafts rather than retroactively rewriting them.
